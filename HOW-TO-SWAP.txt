Portfolio v2: how to put it live
================================

COPY, DON'T CUT
Keep this portfolio-v2 folder as it is. It's your working copy and your
backup. Copy files out of it; never move them.

1. Open your live site folder (the one connected to Netlify / GitHub).
2. Copy these from portfolio-v2 into it, replacing the old ones:
     index.html
     me.html
     lumi.html
     novel-office.html
     meeting-room.html
     assets/v2   (the whole folder, goes inside your existing assets folder)
3. Do NOT copy:
     novel-signature-homes.html   (hidden for now)
     HOW-TO-SWAP.txt
4. Keep your old assets folder (Lumi Screens, Novel Office, Meeting room,
   Site wide images, the resume PDF). The new pages load images from there.
5. Old pages: if your live folder has lumi/, novel-office/ or meeting-room/
   folders, or an old about page, delete them so they don't clash with the
   new .html files.
6. Commit and push. Netlify deploys on its own.

SAFER: CHECK BEFORE IT GOES LIVE
Push to a new branch (for example "v2") and open a pull request.
Netlify gives you a deploy preview link with the real images.
Check it on your phone and laptop, then merge to go live.

GO BACK
Revert the commit (or redeploy the previous deploy in Netlify) and push.
