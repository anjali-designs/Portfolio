NOVEL OFFICE

Novel Office

Redesigning the digital experience for managed workspaces

Novel Office offers managed and flexible workspaces for businesses across Bengaluru. I worked on redesigning the digital experience around how people explore these spaces, from restructuring the information architecture and navigation to making the content easier to scan, compare, and understand.

ROLEProduct Designer
SCOPEUX · UI · Responsive Web Design · Interaction
STATUSLive

PROBLEM

Users struggle to understand workspace offerings, compare options, and take action due to unclear navigation, lack of pricing transparency, and overwhelming information.

GOAL

To design a seamless and intuitive website experience that enables users to easily explore and inquire about office spaces.

QUANTITATIVE RESEARCH

What Users Expect When Searching for Office Spaces

A short online survey was conducted with 20 respondents who had previously searched for office spaces, to understand user behavior and expectations.

13/20browse office spaces on mobile
18/20expect to see pricing before contacting
17/20said complex navigation would make them leave a site
19/20prefer a quick enquiry option over a long form

COMPETITOR RESEARCH

WEWORK

AWFIS

BANGALORE OFFICE

OBSERVATIONS

Key project learnings

01

Designing for a brand is more than making it look good.

Novel Office had an established visual identity and a specific audience, so the challenge wasn't simply creating a visually appealing website. I had to translate the brand's personality into an experience that felt clear, trustworthy, and easy to navigate

02

Research helped me design beyond assumptions.

Speaking to users and looking at competitors helped me understand what people actually needed when evaluating an office space, from understanding the spaces available to finding the right information quickly. This helped me prioritize content and structure instead of designing purely from a visual perspective.

03

Information architecture shapes the experience.

Working through the IA made me realize how much a website's usability depends on what comes before the visual design. Organizing spaces, services, locations, and information into a logical structure made the final interface much easier to build and navigate.

04

Designing the system early made the UI more consistent.

Defining typography, colors, components, and visual patterns before building out the pages helped maintain consistency across a large number of screens while still allowing each page to have its own purpose.
