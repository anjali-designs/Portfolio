MEETING ROOM BOOKING SYSTEM

Meeting Room Booking System

A simpler way to find, book, and manage meeting spaces.

A workplace booking experience designed to help users quickly discover suitable meeting rooms, check availability, and complete reservations without unnecessary friction.

ROLEProduct Designer
SCOPEUX · UI · User Research · Interaction
STATUSDemo Phase

OVERVIEW

Making room booking less of a hassle.

In busy workplace environments, finding an available meeting room at the right time shouldn't require a masterclass in coordination. This project aims to bridge the gap between instant availability and structured planning, allowing team members to secure spaces in seconds.

THE CHALLENGE

How do we make booking a meeting room feel quick and predictable?

SIMPLICITY

Users should be able to book a room quickly without navigating through unnecessary steps, complicated forms, or confusing calendar schedules.

INFORMATION

Users still need enough context to confidently choose a room, including real-time daily availability schedules, amenities, seat capacity, and exact floor maps.

“The challenge wasn't simply designing a calendar or booking form. It was designing the decision-making experience around the booking.”
INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE+

Understanding the booking journey

By mapping out precise user actions from first intent to final confirmation, we streamlined the steps required to identify conflicts and secure rooms smoothly.

OBSERVATIONS

Key project learnings

01

Designing the workflow is as important as the UI

Perfect interfaces fall short if the user's booking sequence is disjointed. Mapping alternative error-states and cancellation logic early prevented complex dev handoffs.

02

Good information reduces decision fatigue

We discovered that over-indexing on raw metrics (like square footage) was far less helpful to users than clear contextual cues like seat capacity and noise cancellation labels.

03

Post-booking experiences are often forgotten

The booking process doesn't end with a success toast. Proper coordination with calendar invites, fast calendar updates, and easy booking-releases are what make a workplace seamless.
