ABOUT ME

Hi, I'm Anjali.

I question things.
Then I design them.

I’m curious about how products work, why people use them the way they do, and what happens when the two don’t quite line up.

My favourite part of design is figuring out the why before jumping into the how.

Right now, I’m exploring AI, SaaS, and the new design problems that come with them.

curious by default.

WHAT I CARE ABOUT

01

Clarity over cleverness

Good design doesn't need to make people stop and admire the interface. I care about making it obvious, so users can understand and move forward without having to think too hard.

02

Systems, not just screens

I like thinking beyond individual screens — information architecture, workflows, roles, relationships and reusable patterns are where the interesting problems begin.

03

Make complexity feel manageable

Some products are complicated because the problem itself is complicated. I don't try to remove that complexity — I try to give people the right information, at the right moment, in the right shape.

“Less confusion → more confidence.”

DESIGNING WITH AI

Designing with AI, not
handing design over to AI.

AI has become part of how I explore ideas — from structuring messy content and generating early directions to challenging assumptions and getting through the first few iterations faster.

I use AI as a thinking partner, accelerator and sometimes a very patient rubber duck.

But the important questions still belong to the designer:

  • Why does this exist?
  • Who is it for?
  • What are they trying to do?
  • What could go wrong?

The tool changes.
The questions don't.

“AI as a copilot”
